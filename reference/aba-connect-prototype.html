ITP drafting reminder
While a client's ITP is being drafted, you'll get reminded at 1 week and 2 weeks in, so it's never forgotten. ITPs can take longer than expected — the toggle below decides what happens after that second reminder.
Keep reminding weekly after the 2nd notice
When on, if the ITP still isn't marked received after the 2-week reminder, you'll get a new reminder every week — for as long as it takes — until it's resolved. When off, reminders stop after the 2-week notice and nothing further is sent.
Currently on — an overdue ITP will keep getting a weekly reminder indefinitely, until it's marked received.
